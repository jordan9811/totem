## Leading pick so far

**2026-10-23: the owner is on RHIZA** (see its row above). Said "REE-za" (the Greek), spelled Rhiza. The
pronunciation choice barely matters legally: a non-English word has no "correct" pronunciation for an examiner (In re
Viterra, Fed. Cir. 2012), so the RIZA sound-alike is a moderate risk either way, for the lawyer to weigh.
