# Post as you

"Allow Totem to post as you": the Post box posts as the Farcaster account you already have, with a key made in your browser, kept in your vault and approved by you in the Farcaster app. No Neynar and no managed signer.

Every test runs against local stand-ins of Farcaster's signed key request API and of a Snapchain node. The owner's first real approval and post on ontotem.com is the test that would change this status. The verdict: your posting key is yours. It is random, made in the tab, sealed in your own vault before anyone else hears of it, and used only in your browser; no server holds it, and it shares nothing with any persona's keys. Totem's validator does two jobs it cannot avoid: it signs the key request with Totem's app account (the account every request must name), and it passes your already-signed posts on to a Farcaster node, because the node refuses posts straight from a browser. So the validator sees your posts (public a moment later anyway) and your internet address, and adding the key publishes on the blockchain that your account uses Totem. {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "35. Post as you: your own Farcaster account's key, Totem's app account, and what each party sees"}

## At a glance

<ol class="flow">
  <li class="flow__us"><strong>Make the key</strong>An Ed25519 key, made in this tab and sealed in the <code>rootSigner</code> vault record, 1,024 bytes, before any request.</li>
  <li><strong>Ask</strong>The validator signs a SignedKeyRequest with Totem's app account and creates the request with Farcaster's API.</li>
  <li><strong>Approve</strong>You open the link on a phone, or scan the QR code on a computer, and approve Totem in the Farcaster app.</li>
  <li class="flow__us"><strong>Check</strong>Your account must be the one that approved, and the Farcaster node your browser reads must list the key as active.</li>
  <li class="flow__us"><strong>Post</strong>Each post is signed in the tab and passed on, unchanged, by the validator's relay: "Posts go to Farcaster as @you."</li>
</ol>
<p class="flow__note">Nothing is turned on before the check passes. Every refusal is a fixed sentence, and a post the node refuses is an error with the node's own words.</p>

## The flow, step by step

1. **Start.** You are signed in with Farcaster ([Sign in with Farcaster](/mechanism/sign-in-with-farcaster)), and the site's validator says its app account is ready (`GET /fc/app-account`). Pressing **Allow Totem to post as you** makes the key and seals it as "pending" first. Only then does the browser send the PUBLIC key to the validator (`POST /fc/key-request`).
2. **The key request.** The validator signs the EIP-712 SignedKeyRequest (`requestFid` = Totem's app fid, `key` = your public key, `deadline` = 15 minutes by default) with the app account's custody key, and posts it to Farcaster's API. It answers with Farcaster's token and approval link. The browser checks that answer before showing anything: its own key, the app fid the validator publishes, a deadline in the future and at most a day away, and a link on the Farcaster app's own scheme or web hosts. Anything else is refused and no link is shown.
3. **Approve.** On a phone, **Open the Farcaster app**; on a computer, a QR code to scan with the phone's camera. The panel says, before the first request: "Totem makes a posting key in this browser and keeps it in your vault; you approve it in the Farcaster app. The Farcaster app may ask you to pay a small network fee for adding it. Totem's server signs the request and passes your posts on to Farcaster, so it sees them (they're public anyway) and your internet address. Anonymous posts from this browser reach it from the same address: post those from another network if you want them kept apart."
4. **Poll.** Every 3 seconds the browser asks the validator (`GET /fc/key-request?token=`), which asks Farcaster. "approved" means the transaction is on its way; "completed" means it landed. The key is turned on only when the approving account (`userFid`) IS your signed-in account, and the Snapchain node your browser reads directly lists it as an active signer of that account (`GET /v1/onChainSignersByFid`). An approval by another account in your Farcaster app is refused and the key forgotten. A key the node never lists is refused five minutes after "completed". {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "35. Post as you: your own Farcaster account's key, Totem's app account, and what each party sees"}
5. **Post.** The composer becomes yours: "Posting as @you", then "Posts go to Farcaster as @you." and a note about the main Farcaster app (below). Each post is a Farcaster message built and signed in the tab, sent to the validator's relay (`POST /fc/hub/submit`), which forwards the bytes unchanged to the node's `POST /v1/submitMessage`. Casts and deletes come from the Post box; replies, likes and recasts (and their undo), and Delete on your own posts, come from the Farcaster cards in [Following](/mechanism/following-from-the-hub), threads and profiles. A card's button is pressed only after the node accepted the message, and a refusal is a sentence on the card. {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "35. Post as you: your own Farcaster account's key, Totem's app account, and what each party sees"}

**Only approve a request you started.** Anyone can ask Totem for a request for a key they hold, so a link or QR code someone *sends* you could put their key on your account. The panel says so under the code: "Only approve a request you started here. If someone sent you a link or a code to approve, don't." A request lasts 15 minutes. If you approved one by mistake, remove it in the Farcaster app (Settings, Connected apps).

While you act as a persona, nothing goes out as your own account: no post, like, recast or reply, refused in your browser before anything is signed, so the persona's Post box does not offer posting as you and the cards say why. Nothing as your own account ever likes, recasts or replies to one of your personas' posts either. To act as your own account, choose **You** in the account menu (built on test data only, not deployed): then no persona acts, the Post box and the cards act as you, your own modes, rules, lenses, rings, imports and anonymous posts work as they do with no persona, and only what would speak as a persona is refused until you switch back; a reload goes back to your first persona. Settings › Account keeps the panel and **Disconnect**. {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "35. Post as you: your own Farcaster account's key, Totem's app account, and what each party sees"}

## The contracts it speaks

Checked against the documentation and, read-only, against the live services on 2026-11-05.

| What | The contract |
|---|---|
| The SignedKeyRequest | EIP-712, domain `Farcaster SignedKeyRequestValidator`, version `1`, chain `10`, verifying contract `0x00000000FC700472606ED4fA22623Acf62c60553`; type `SignedKeyRequest(uint256 requestFid,bytes key,uint256 deadline)`. Signed on the validator with `@noble/curves` (the validator image has no viem), byte-identical to viem's in the tests. [Signed Key Request Validator](https://docs.neynar.com/farcaster/reference/contracts/reference/signed-key-request-validator) |
| Creating the request | `POST https://api.farcaster.xyz/v2/signed-key-requests` with `{ key, requestFid, signature, deadline }` (and an optional `sponsorship`); the answer is `result.signedKeyRequest` with `token`, `deeplinkUrl`, `key` and `state: "pending"`. [Signer requests](https://docs.neynar.com/farcaster/reference/farcaster/signer-requests) (the Farcaster docs, mirrored by Neynar) |
| Polling it | `GET https://api.farcaster.xyz/v2/signed-key-request?token=…`: `pending`, `approved` (with `userFid`) or `completed` (with `userFid`). An unknown token answers 400, "No signed key request found", with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` |
| Checking the key | A Snapchain node's `GET /v1/onChainSignersByFid?fid=…&signer=0x…`, read from the browser (the public node `https://snap.farcaster.xyz:3381` answers browser reads with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`) |
| Posting | The node's `POST /v1/submitMessage` takes only `Content-Type: application/octet-stream` and answers a browser's preflight with 404, so the browser cannot post to it directly ([snapchain `src/network/http_server.rs`](https://github.com/farcasterxyz/snapchain/blob/main/src/network/http_server.rs); A-ROOTPOST-CORS {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "35. Post as you: your own Farcaster account's key, Totem's app account, and what each party sees"}). The messages are `CastAdd` (with a reply's `parent_cast_id`), `CastRemove`, `ReactionAdd` and `ReactionRemove`, field numbers from [snapchain `proto/definitions/message.proto`](https://github.com/farcasterxyz/snapchain/blob/main/proto/definitions/message.proto), hashed with BLAKE3 and signed with Ed25519 |
| The app account's registration | `IdGateway.registerFor(to, recovery, deadline, sig)` with an EIP-712 `Register(address to,address recovery,uint256 nonce,uint256 deadline)` signed by the new account, paid by the caller. [ID Gateway](https://docs.neynar.com/farcaster/reference/contracts/reference/id-gateway) |
| The app account's profile key | `KeyGateway.addFor(fidOwner, keyType, key, metadataType, metadata, deadline, sig)` with an EIP-712 `Add(address owner,uint32 keyType,bytes key,uint8 metadataType,bytes metadata,uint256 nonce,uint256 deadline)` signed by the app account's custody (domain `Farcaster KeyGateway`, version `1`, chain `10`; the nonce is `KeyGateway.nonces(owner)`), sent and paid by any wallet; the metadata is a SignedKeyRequest the custody signs for its own fid. Checked read-only on OP Mainnet on 2026-11-05: the typehash, the domain separator and the typed-data hash match. [Key Gateway](https://docs.neynar.com/farcaster/reference/contracts/reference/key-gateway) |
| The app account's name | `POST https://fnames.farcaster.xyz/transfers` with a UserNameProof signed by the custody, then the `USERNAME`, `DISPLAY`, `BIO` and `PFP` UserData messages, signed by the profile key. [Fname registry](https://docs.neynar.com/farcaster/reference/fname/api) |

The relay checks a message before forwarding it (a Farcaster message, BLAKE3 and Ed25519, its hash and signature valid, Farcaster mainnet, one of the four types) and refuses the rest without forwarding. It reads; it never rewrites, and it cannot forge: the node checks every signature against the chain. {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "35. Post as you: your own Farcaster account's key, Totem's app account, and what each party sees"}

## Who pays to add the key

The Farcaster app sends the transaction that adds your key. Farcaster's documentation says an app "can sponsor a signer so that the user doesn't need to pay" when the app is signed up in the Farcaster app with at least 100 warps. Totem's app account has a profile (@ontotem) but is not signed up there, so for now the Farcaster app may ask YOU for the network fee, and the panel says so before the request. The gas itself is under a tenth of a cent on OP Mainnet; whatever the Farcaster app charges on top is its own and is not published. Sponsorship is built behind `FC_APP_SPONSOR=on` for when the app account is signed up, and the panel then says "Totem pays the network fee for adding it." {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "35. Post as you: your own Farcaster account's key, Totem's app account, and what each party sees"}

## What each party sees

| Party | Sees |
|---|---|
| Totem's validator | the public key of each request, its time and your internet address; each poll, and once approved your account number; every post, like, recast and reply it passes on (so which Farcaster post you acted on), with your address and the time |
| Farcaster's API | the public key, Totem's app fid, the deadline and signature, from the validator's address (your browser does not call it); then, from your Farcaster app, who approved |
| Everyone, on the blockchain | that your account added a key requested by Totem's app fid, and when |
| The Farcaster node your browser reads | your address asking whether your account lists the key, and each post through the relay |
| Totem's app server | nothing: the route runs in your tab and answers any server `404 device_only` before reading the body |

Never, to any of them: the private key, your Totem identity, or your personas. {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "35. Post as you: your own Farcaster account's key, Totem's app account, and what each party sees"}

The residual worth knowing: your anonymous posts reach the same validator from the same internet address while no outside relay exists, so the operator could match an anonymous post to your own account by address and hour, and every post as you gives it a fresh match (A-ROOTPOST-SAME-ADDRESS). The panel says so before the first request. {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "35. Post as you: your own Farcaster account's key, Totem's app account, and what each party sees"}

## Your personas stay apart

The key is not derived from your seed and shares no derivation with any persona key, so posting as you cannot use or reveal a persona's key. The persona routes still never act as your own account: a persona's buttons answer "Persona actions never act as your Farcaster account. To act as yourself, choose You in the account menu: then the Post box and the Like, Recast and Reply on Farcaster's posts act as your account, once you allow Totem to post as you. To do this as a persona, switch to one of your personas." and forking from it stays refused. {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "35. Post as you: your own Farcaster account's key, Totem's app account, and what each party sees"} {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "21. Sign in with Farcaster on the public site: what the relay, the chain node, the server and the validator learn"}

## Disconnect

Settings › Account › Post as you, type DISCONNECT. Totem forgets the key on this identity; the sealed record keeps only its public half, so the page can still say what to remove. What actually stops the key is the Farcaster app: open Settings, then Connected apps, and remove Totem. Until you do, the key can still post, and a copy of your vault from before the disconnect, opened with your seed, still holds it. An identity you never saved loses the key when the tab closes; the key stays on your account until you remove it in the Farcaster app. {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "35. Post as you: your own Farcaster account's key, Totem's app account, and what each party sees"}

## The main Farcaster app

The composer says: "Heads up: the main Farcaster app now hides new posts from accounts that haven't accepted its rules (Network School's "social contract"). Your posts still show everywhere else." That is Farcaster's rule since its sale to Network School in October 2026, not Totem's.

## Totem's app account (the owner's)

Every key request names an app account, so Totem has one of its own, named **@ontotem**: the Farcaster app's approval screen shows the requesting app's profile, so approving reads as approving Totem rather than a number. `scripts/owner/make-app-account.ps1` makes it once on the owner's laptop, saves its two keys in `%USERPROFILE%\.totem-app\app-account.json` (never printed), registers it with `IdGateway.registerFor` and adds its profile key with `KeyGateway.addFor`, both paid by the @anonwire account's custody after one CONFIRM: about $0.20 for the registration plus well under a cent of gas for each, about $0.22 at most, and no ETH moves between the wallets. Then, free, it registers the name and publishes "Totem", "ontotem.com" and the picture. `scripts/owner/arm-app-account.ps1` then puts `FC_APP_FID` and `FC_APP_CUSTODY_KEY` on the public validator only; the public app refuses the key at boot. On the validator the custody key signs key requests and nothing else, so whoever holds it there cannot post as any visitor. The profile key stays on the owner's laptop: it can post as @ontotem and cannot touch any visitor; removing it on chain (`KeyRegistry.remove`) takes the profile with it. A leaked custody key: `arm-app-account.ps1 -Disarm`, then a new app account armed with `-Replace`; keys visitors already approved keep working. {cite: SECURITY-POSTURE.md > "35. Post as you: your own Farcaster account's key, Totem's app account, and what each party sees"}

## Where it lives, and how it is tested

- **Browser:** `lib/fc/rootSigner/flow.ts` (the flow and its sentences), `store.ts` (the sealed key), `config.ts` (the node rule), `copy.ts` (the words), `lib/fc/hub/message.ts` (the messages), `app/api/fc/root/route.ts`, `components/root/RootPost.tsx`, the composer and Settings › Account.
- **Validator:** `validator/keyrequest.js` (the app account, the key requests, the relay), `validator/eip712.js` (the signature).
- **Owner:** `scripts/owner/make-app-account.ps1` / `.mjs`, `scripts/owner/arm-app-account.ps1`.
- **Tests:** `npm run test:root-post-unit` (the browser flow end to end against the real validator and the stand-ins, the browser's messages byte-identical to the validator's, a card's like, recast, reply and undo arriving at the relay as exactly the bytes your key signs, nothing sent while a persona acts or aimed at one of your personas, a mutation of every guard), `scripts/accept-hub-read-prod.mjs` (the same from real cards in a browser, and switching to You and back), `npm run test:acting-root-unit` (acting as yourself: no persona write while no persona acts, the switch, boot, and a queued write or read stopped by a switch), `validator/test/keyrequest.test.js` in `npm run test:validator` (a wrong requestFid and an expired deadline refused by the stand-in Farcaster API, the per-address cap, the relay's refusals), `npm run test:app-account` and the owner harness.
