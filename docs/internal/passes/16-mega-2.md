## What it found

- The reviewers' worst finding was CRITICAL and was the pass's own design: the DM bookmark hash was salted with the ROOT, so two personas bookmarking the same tweet shared a hash, joinable in the plain folder rows and in readwise's plaintext record keys; the fix folds the persona into the keyed message and makes readwise one opaque record. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 7 — Linkage test v5 + adversarial review"}
- A second reviewer found the whole mode definition — the action-button slots included — was a plaintext, persona-keyed habit fingerprint the operator could cluster across a root's personas; `modes` became a sealed namespace like the zone config, and the other preference namespaces that are still plain were carried as a named assumption and an open issue rather than quietly. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 7 — Linkage test v5 + adversarial review"}
- A pending relationship port resolved to whoever later attested the handle, with no re-confirmation and no expiry; the pass shipped a 30-day lapse and left the re-confirmation step open, which is exactly the shape of a half-fix and was recorded as one. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 7 — Linkage test v5 + adversarial review"}
- The `stewardship` rep events were stamped with the millisecond while the posture claimed hour grain, and the first run of the fixed suite failed on seven legacy rows already on disk — the table is the claim, not the new write, so a migration coarsened them. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 7 — Linkage test v5 + adversarial review"}
- One review finding corrected the POSTURE rather than the code: the history gate is the app's query path and the validator's own per-cast stake read is ungated by design, so the overstatement was rewritten instead of a gate being invented to match it. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 7 — Linkage test v5 + adversarial review"}
- Gate 1's only real product failure was an identity mismatch the suite caught: the accrual named the seed's INTERNAL root id while the validator knows the account by the id it minted at registration, and the fix was to resolve the root exactly as registration does and to have the suite read it from the operator's own table instead of assuming the seed's name. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 1 — Reputation UBI (the backbone; single-threaded)"}
- The register of modules that make the proof-mediated tier read was incomplete, and the first run of the root-reads suite caught the omission before the commit. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 1 — Reputation UBI (the backbone; single-threaded)"}

## Judgment calls

- No dwell, anywhere in accrual: the relationship event log bars dwell and impression events by construction, and rewarding "how long people looked" would need exactly the surveillance that log exists to refuse, so accrual uses non-retraction, community notes and standing instead. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "Standing judgment calls (this pass)"}
- Where the owner's design intent was fuzzy — the early-to-content curve, the creator-economy tie-in — the build is the conservative parameterized version marked `// TUNE` in the source and listed on the site's open-issues page, rather than a guess dressed as a mechanic. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "Standing judgment calls (this pass)"}
- Less claimed, more refused, more logged by id, wherever anything else was undecidable. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "Standing judgment calls (this pass)"}
- The validator cannot tell an over-spent stake from an honest one and does not try: it verifies a signature under the key it is given and carries it, while the READER re-verifies every row against the key it resolves and treats an unverifiable row as absent. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 1 — Reputation UBI (the backbone; single-threaded)"}
- A refused stake is never a failed like: the action stands on its own and the stake result is reported beside it, because tying the two would make a depleted allowance silently break the product's ordinary verbs. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 1 — Reputation UBI (the backbone; single-threaded)"}
- Stakes route additively into the notes and refutation surfaces and the STANDING rule is untouched — volume still counts for nothing there, because a stake is signal, not a gate. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 1 — Reputation UBI (the backbone; single-threaded)"}
- Coordination is detectable, not enforced: clusters are computed on the public rows and rendered, nothing refuses or punishes, and a LENS may discount them — legibility made actionable by whoever writes the lens rather than by the platform. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 2 — Lenses: the algo through different curves (single-threaded)"}
- The canonical lens is the identity — every weight zero, the canonical curves, the feed byte-for-byte what it was without one — so "view the algo through a different lens" is literally a field on the mode and nothing about the default changed. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 2 — Lenses: the algo through different curves (single-threaded)"}
- A lens section that had to be stripped is no longer what its author signed, so the import refuses on the signature rather than quietly importing a weakened version; the preview still names every stripped field, so "why will this not import" is never a mystery. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 2 — Lenses: the algo through different curves (single-threaded)"}
- The `dwell` signal a lens can weight is the reading-time DEMAND of the content — its length and its long-form flag — never measured viewer attention, which keeps the seeded "Slow reads" lens honest about what it is weighting. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 2 — Lenses: the algo through different curves (single-threaded)"}
- The extra button bindings are one-tap only where a one-tap API already exists and open the relevant SURFACE otherwise, named as such in the report rather than faked as one-taps. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 3 — Scroll zones + configurable action buttons"}
- Real-device scroll behaviour is declared UNMEASURED rather than inferred from Playwright's synthetic wheel and touch, and tuning on a phone is listed as required work. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 3 — Scroll zones + configurable action buttons"}
- The local model is called a lexicon and not a neural encoder: a MiniLM-class model is a drop-in behind the same interface, and shipping one means shipping weights, which this pass does not do. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 4 — Unified search (vault-local, keyword + AI)"}
- When the device cannot run the model the box degrades to keyword and says so in words on the page, rather than returning worse results under the same label. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 4 — Unified search (vault-local, keyword + AI)"}
- An unlinked DM sender gets the funnel reply and nothing at all is kept — not the DM, not the handle, not the link — because linking is the funnel and storing the approach would make the funnel a collection. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 5 — @layers on X: the DM-to-bookmark hook"}
- A port is a grant, not a self-claim: alt-linking's `from === to` shape was a claim to one audience member, and porting a relationship means issuing the real grant, whitelisted to the counterpart. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 6 — Relationship porting on X (extends alt-linking)"}
- A private persona is refused in either role of a port, because a private persona's linkage is never carried anywhere by anything. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 6 — Relationship porting on X (extends alt-linking)"}
- Co-timing is measured and published rather than suppressed: two personas of one root staking in the same hour is a behavioural link, so the test computes the candidate set, prints it, and fails only if the two would be alone in their hour. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 7 — Linkage test v5 + adversarial review"}
- "Opaque" is defined as not derivable from what the operator holds, so a hash the operator could brute-force from tweet ids alone fails the intent even when it passes the shape check — which is why the derivation carries a vault-key salt and why a reviewer was pointed at exactly it. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 7 — Linkage test v5 + adversarial review"}
- VitePress over the alternatives for a stated reason: mdBook needs a Rust toolchain the machine does not have, Docusaurus would be a second React app, and markdown-native means the check tooling and the site read one tree. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 8 — Docs site: scaffold, sections, piping, historical pages"}
- The docs check's keyword list is deliberately about CLAIMS rather than topics — "private persona" is a feature name, not an assertion — and hand-written prose cites while generated pages do not. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 8 — Docs site: scaffold, sections, piping, historical pages"}
- Internal pages are hidden by excluding them from the source set rather than by unlinking them, and public pages are forbidden from linking inward, so hiding is clean rather than a 404. {cite: notes/reports/REPORT-MEGA2.md > "GATE 8 — Docs site: scaffold, sections, piping, historical pages"}
